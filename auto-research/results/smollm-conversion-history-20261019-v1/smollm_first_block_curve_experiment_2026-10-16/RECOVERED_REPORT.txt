SmolLM2: one block, exact curve representation

NUMERICAL EQUIVALENCE PASSED


The first transformer block now reads all of its parameters from a single cubic curve. The other 29 blocks remain unchanged. Every tested logit matches the original bit for bit.




What this establishes


A lossless conversion and executable inverse are working for a pretrained language model. Seven projection matrices and two RMSNorm vectors map to one curve with 
3,540,096 scalar knots
. The saved curve was reloaded before constructing the tested candidate.



Scope:
 this is a parameter representation. Attention, RoPE, normalization, feed-forward nonlinearities, and residual connections retain their original computation. Curve position indexes stored parameter entries; it does not index depth or time.


The checkpoint is 
HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M
, revision 
93efa2f097d58c2a74874c7e644dbc9b0cee75a2
. Its 134,515,008 learned parameters retain all their information. Both models were evaluated in FP32, widening the source BF16 values exactly. No training or quantization was performed.

The curve participates in computation


Moving the query-projection sampling coordinates by +0.125 of one knot interval, with all other tensors unchanged, produces a maximum first-block output change of 
0.256037
 and a maximum logit change of 
0.136566
. Returning to the original integer coordinates restores bitwise equality. Argmax tokens stayed unchanged on this particular prompt.


This control demonstrates that the curve evaluation is active. It does not demonstrate a useful semantic direction or better model quality.


Why the conversion is exact

Cθ(j) = θ[j]                       at every integer knot j
R(Cθ) = θ                          recover original tensors
Fcurve(x, cache) = Foriginal(x, cache)   preserve the computation graph


The interpolant is the C1 uniform Catmull–Rom cubic with implicit central-difference slopes and clamped endpoint neighbors. Once that rule is fixed, the parameter vector and its curve have a one-to-one correspondence. Off-knot geometry is a chosen extension. Other interpolation rules could pass through the same stored values.

Cost and timing


The curve stores 
14.16 MB
 of FP32 values, equal to the original block in FP32. The original BF16 values occupy 7.08 MB. There is no compression. At exact knots, evaluating the cardinal basis simplifies to a direct view into the ordinate array. Matrix multiplication still performs the projection work.

Limits and next useful question


The construction retains every degree of freedom. Scalar flattening is arbitrary, so visual smoothness between neighboring coefficients is not evidence of a low-dimensional learned structure. This experiment does not establish improved quality, faster inference, smaller memory, trainability, or continuous-depth behavior.


The exact conversion is now a control for testing better coordinate choices and the functional effect of curve edits, still within the first block. TinyLlama was not modified or downloaded in this experiment.
