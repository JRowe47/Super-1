WikiText-2 raw selections
=======================
Source dataset: Salesforce/wikitext, wikitext-2-raw-v1
Pinned revision: b08601e04326c79dfdd32d625aee71d232d685c3
https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext/tree/b08601e04326c79dfdd32d625aee71d232d685c3/wikitext-2-raw-v1
Dataset authors: Stephen Merity, Caiming Xiong, James Bradbury, Richard Socher.
Underlying text: Wikipedia contributors.
Dataset reference: Pointer Sentinel Mixture Models, https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.07843
Dataset card lists CC BY-SA 3.0 and GFDL:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
https://www.gnu.org/licenses/fdl-1.3.html

The bundled data/phase_blocks.npz contains tokenized, selected 96-token
excerpts, not the full dataset. These selections remain subject to their
source licenses. No ownership of the underlying text is claimed. Source row
indices, offsets, and full-row text hashes are in data/evaluation_selection.json and provenance/*selection*.json.
Source parquet hashes and immutable download URLs are in data/source.json.
The selections were tokenized with the bundled Pythia tokenizer. Training
and validation selections are distinct source splits; absence from Pythia's
pretraining corpus has not been established.

Pythia model
============
EleutherAI/pythia-70m, step143000
https://huggingface.co/EleutherAI/pythia-70m/tree/de3e4e2d6cbb3b1a51f90fe153ea51fcd8c7c852
Base weights are not redistributed in this bundle; download_sources.py
retrieves the pinned file and verifies its hash. Configuration and tokenizer
files are bundled with hashes. The two adapted decoder NPZ files derive from
that model and should be used subject to its model license (Apache 2.0):
https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

Experimental probes
===================
Synthetic copying inputs and Gaussian ring-routing examples were generated
for these experiments. The original eight prose probes and token IDs are
retained from the preceding phase in results/probe_inputs.json. They are a
reused retention diagnostic, not a new held-out language benchmark.
